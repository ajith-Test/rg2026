What is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by many employers and recruiting teams to help collect, organize, search, and manage job applications.

ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System.

It is a recruiting software system that helps companies manage applications and candidate information. Depending on the system and employer's workflow, resumes may be parsed, searched, filtered, ranked, or reviewed using different criteria.


UNDERSTANDING ATS

What Does an ATS Actually Do?

An ATS can support recruiters throughout different stages of the hiring process.

📥

Collects Applications

Job applications and resumes can be collected and organized in one recruiting system.

🔎

Searches Candidate Data

Recruiters may search candidate profiles and resumes using skills, experience, education, keywords, and other information.

🗂️

Organizes Candidates

Candidate information can be organized into different stages of the recruitment process.

⚡

Supports Screening

Some ATS workflows can help recruiters identify candidates based on information relevant to a job opening.

ATS MYTHS

3 Things People Often Get Wrong About ATS

MYTH 01

"ATS automatically rejects every resume below a score."

Not necessarily. ATS platforms and employer workflows vary. Some systems provide search, parsing, filtering, or ranking capabilities, while recruiters may use the information differently.

MYTH 02

"Adding as many keywords as possible is best."

No. Keywords should be relevant and truthful. Keyword stuffing can make your resume difficult to read and does not replace genuine skills or experience.

MYTH 03

"ATS-friendly means making your resume for robots."

Not really. A good resume should be easy for systems to process and easy for recruiters to read. Human readability still matters.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ATS Questions, Answered.

What does ATS stand for?

ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System. It is software used by employers and recruiting teams to manage job applications and candidate information.

Does every company use an ATS?

No. Companies use different recruiting tools and processes, and some may not use an ATS at all.

Can an ATS read a PDF resume?

Many modern recruiting systems can process PDF resumes, but compatibility can vary. Always follow the file format instructions provided in the job application.

What is an ATS-friendly resume?

An ATS-friendly resume uses clear structure, readable formatting, relevant job-specific terminology, and properly organized information so that both recruiting software and humans can understand it more easily.

Does an ATS score guarantee an interview?

No. An ATS score is only an assessment tool. Hiring decisions depend on many factors, including your qualifications, experience, the job requirements, recruiter review, and the employer's hiring process.

How can I improve my ATS resume?

Start with clear section headings, relevant skills and keywords, measurable achievements, readable formatting, and content tailored to the specific job you are applying for.

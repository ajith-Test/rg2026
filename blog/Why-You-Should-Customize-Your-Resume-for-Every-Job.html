Why You Should Customize Your Resume for Every Job

Sending exactly the same resume to every employer may save time, but it can make your application less relevant to the position.

Different Jobs Have Different Requirements

Even two positions with similar job titles can require different skills, tools and responsibilities.

Start With the Job Description

Identify the most important requirements and compare them with your experience.

Customize Your Summary

Adjust your professional summary so that it reflects the role you are targeting.

Prioritize Relevant Skills

Move the most relevant skills into focus while keeping your resume truthful.

Adjust Your Experience

Highlight achievements and responsibilities that are most relevant to the position.

Customization does not mean rewriting your entire resume every time. Focus on the sections that matter most for the specific job.

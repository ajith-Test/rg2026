How AI Can Help You Build a Better Resume

Artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly useful in career preparation. AI-powered tools can help job seekers organize information, improve wording and identify potential gaps in their resumes.

Generate Better Resume Content

AI can help turn basic notes about your experience into clearer, professional statements.

Identify Missing Skills

By comparing your current skills with a target role, AI tools can help you identify areas where additional learning may be useful.

Improve Your Resume Language

AI can help identify repetitive language, unclear statements and areas where your achievements could be communicated more effectively.

Analyze Job Descriptions

AI can help you understand the important requirements in a job description and identify relevant skills or terminology to consider.

AI Should Support Your Experience — Not Invent It

One of the most important rules when using AI for your resume is accuracy. Never allow an AI tool to add qualifications, skills, responsibilities or achievements that you do not actually have.

Use AI to improve how you communicate your real experience, not to create experience that you don't have.

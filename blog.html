Resume & ATS FAQs

What is an ATS-friendly resume?

An ATS-friendly resume uses clear structure, readable formatting and relevant content that can be understood by both recruitment software and human recruiters.

How can I improve my ATS resume score?

Review the job description, include relevant keywords that genuinely match your experience, strengthen your skills and experience sections, and maintain a clear resume structure.

Can freshers create an ATS-friendly resume?

Yes. Freshers can create an ATS-friendly resume by focusing on relevant education, skills, projects, internships, certifications and achievements.

Should I customize my resume for every job?

Customizing relevant sections of your resume can make your application more closely aligned with the requirements of each position.

Can AI write my entire resume?

AI can help you draft and improve resume content, but you should always review the final resume and make sure every statement accurately represents your real experience.

Does an ATS score guarantee an interview?

No. An ATS score is an assessment or compatibility indicator. Hiring decisions depend on many factors, including your qualifications, experience, application quality and the employer's hiring process.

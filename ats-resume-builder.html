WHY ATS MATTERS

Your Resume May Be Rejected Before a Recruiter Reads It

Many companies use Applicant Tracking Systems to organize and filter applications. If your resume is difficult for the system to read or doesn't contain relevant information, it may receive less attention during the screening process.

🔍

Missing Keywords

Your resume may not clearly match the skills and keywords employers are looking for in the job description.

📄

Complex Formatting

Complicated layouts, excessive graphics, and inconsistent formatting can make resume information harder to process.

⚠️

Poorly Structured Content

Unclear headings, weak descriptions, and poorly organized sections can make important information harder to identify.
